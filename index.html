CarryCup: Reusable Bottle Holder

Customer problem: students carrying a laptop, books and a reusable bottle struggle to hold everything, so bottles get left behind. Concept: a bottle holder that attaches to a backpack strap. Two versions are compared below.

⚠Interactive concept prototype. Dimensions and costs are assumptions. Physical fit, retention, and durability have not been tested.
